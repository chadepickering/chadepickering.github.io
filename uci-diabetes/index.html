Preface

This case study applies a full-stack analytical pipeline to the UCI 130-US Hospitals Diabetes dataset — a longitudinal collection of 101,766 inpatient encounters from 130 US hospitals spanning 1999–2008. The outcome of interest is 30-day hospital readmission, a high-cost, partially preventable event targeted by the 2010 Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP).

The work is organized in two parts:

Part 1 — Statistical Analysis begins with rigorous exploratory data analysis, causal inference (AIPW, IPTW, PSM) to estimate the effect of HbA1c testing etc. on readmission, and a prospective experimental design including power analysis for a model deployment trial.

Part 2 — Machine Learning Evaluation covers the full modeling pipeline: four competing classifiers (Logistic Regression, XGBoost, LightGBM, TF/Keras), hyperparameter optimization via Optuna, probability calibration, model selection, holdout evaluation, SHAP-based interpretability, subgroup and fairness analysis, and simulated production drift monitoring.

Dataset

  • Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository — Diabetes 130-US Hospitals (Strack et al., 2014)
  • Analytic sample: 69,584 encounters after deduplication and exclusion criteria
  • Outcome: 30-day hospital readmission (binary), ~7% positive rate
  • Period: 1999–2008 across 130 US hospitals

Selected Results

  • Deployed model: XGBoost (AUC-ROC 0.687 holdout, calibrated, τ = 0.12)
  • Flag rate: ~15% of encounters flagged for intervention

  • Drift: No alerts across four simulated semi-annual deployment periods

## Selected Results

- **Deployed model:** XGBoost (AUC-ROC 0.687 holdout, calibrated, τ = 0.12)
- **Flag rate:** ~15% of encounters flagged for intervention
- **Causal anchor:** HbA1c testing reduces readmission (AIPW RR ~0.92), consistent with SHAP directions and peer-reviewed literature
- **Drift:** No alerts across four simulated semi-annual deployment periods
